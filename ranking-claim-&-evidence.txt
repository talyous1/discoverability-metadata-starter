Ranking claim:
A common claim is that stuffing keywords into the meta description will boost your ranking factor. Google has said that meta descriptions aren't a ranking factor. I did not stuff the meta description with keywords for this website. Meta descriptions mainly affect the snippet people see in results, which is why the one I wrote describes what's on the page instead.
